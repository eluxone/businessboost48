Privacy · Updated 22 August 2026

Privacy, without mystery.

This page explains how the current BusinessBoost48 website handles information when you browse it or ask for a free website review.

01

What is collected

This website does not currently use analytics cookies or store the review form on a website database.

The review form opens WhatsApp with a prepared message. Nothing is sent until you review it and press send. The message may include your name, website, stated goal and any email address you choose to enter.

If you use the AI website adviser, the messages you choose to submit are sent through Vercel AI Gateway to the selected model provider so it can generate a reply. BusinessBoost48 does not intentionally save the conversation to a customer database; those providers may process or retain request data under their own service terms.

02

Why it is used

Information you send is used to respond to your enquiry, prepare the review you requested and discuss a possible website project.

It is not sold to third parties.

03

Services involved

AI chat text is processed through Vercel AI Gateway and its selected model provider for the purpose of generating the requested reply.

Review requests and AI handoffs open a prepared message in WhatsApp. WhatsApp/Meta processes anything you choose to send under its own policies and privacy controls. BusinessBoost48 does not receive the message until you press send.

The website's visual typefaces are hosted directly on this domain rather than loaded from a third-party font service. The Google Search Console verification file on this domain proves ownership; it does not itself track your visit.

04

Your choice

Do not enter passwords, payment details, health information or other sensitive information into the AI adviser. Its guidance is automated and is not a binding quote or professional advice.

You can ask for information sent directly to BusinessBoost48 to be corrected or deleted by messaging us on WhatsApp at +44 7438 893486. If the way this website collects information changes, this notice will be updated.
