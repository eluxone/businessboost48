Privacy · Updated 21 August 2026

Privacy, without mystery.

This page explains how the current BusinessBoost48 website handles information when you browse it or ask for a free website review.

01

What is collected

This website does not currently use analytics cookies or store the review form on a website database.

The review form opens your own email app. Nothing is sent until you choose to send that email. The message may include your website, your stated goal and the contact information included by your email provider.

02

Why it is used

Information you send is used to respond to your enquiry, prepare the review you requested and discuss a possible website project.

It is not sold to third parties.

03

Services involved

Your email provider and ours may process the message to deliver it. The website's visual typefaces are hosted directly on this domain rather than loaded from a third-party font service.

The Google Search Console verification file on this domain proves ownership; it does not itself track your visit.

04

Your choice

You can ask for information you sent to be corrected or deleted by emailing hello@businessboost48.co.uk.

If the way this website collects information changes, this notice will be updated.
