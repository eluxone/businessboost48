Website design questions

Clear answers before we build.

Useful websites begin with clarity. These are the questions UK business owners most often ask before choosing BusinessBoost48.

01

Who is BusinessBoost48 for?

We design websites for ambitious UK small businesses, including restaurants, trades, retailers, garages and service companies that need more trust, enquiries or bookings.

02

Can you rebuild an existing website?

Yes. We can review your current message, mobile experience, search foundations and enquiry journey, then rebuild the parts holding the business back.

03

What does AI add to a business website?

Practical AI can recognise customer intent, provide approved guidance and prepare a better human handoff. It should remove friction—not replace your judgement.

04

Is SEO included?

Every build starts with crawlable content, semantic structure, strong metadata and mobile performance. Competitive rankings also depend on useful ongoing content, reputation and relevant links.

05

Can the first version really arrive in 48 hours?

Often, yes—once scope, content and access are confirmed. Larger websites and complex integrations receive a realistic written timeline before work begins.

06

Are the website examples client results?

No. The interactive transformations are clearly labelled demonstration concepts. Genuine client work, reviews and results will only appear when they can be verified.

07

What will my final price include?

Your written scope confirms pages, revisions, content responsibilities, integrations, hosting, support, VAT treatment and any third-party costs before work begins.
