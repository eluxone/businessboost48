What movers need

One website. Several very different intentions.

A buyer, seller, landlord and renter arrive with different questions. The structure should recognise that immediately instead of forcing everyone through the same generic contact page.

01 / Direction

Separate the important journeys.

Buying, selling, letting and renting need clear routes, useful information and calls to action tailored to the person's stage.

02 / Evidence

Make local expertise visible.

Accurate area knowledge, genuine people, clear process and verifiable proof help visitors judge whether the agency understands their move.

03 / Enquiry

Capture useful context.

Valuation and property enquiries should gather enough information for a person to respond well without turning the form into an interrogation.
