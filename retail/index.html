What shoppers need

Less catalogue friction. More buying confidence.

A good retail website helps people narrow the choice, understand the product and trust the business before they reach a basket or enquiry.

01 / Discover

Organise products around how people shop.

Collections, occasions, needs and useful filters can create a clearer route than a long grid of equally weighted products.

02 / Trust

Answer the questions behind the purchase.

Materials, sizing, availability, delivery, returns, care and genuine proof should reduce uncertainty at the point it matters.

03 / Act

Keep the next step focused.

Whether the goal is online purchase, store visit, product enquiry or wholesale conversation, the journey should make it obvious.

Retail website deliverables

A brand experience designed to help people choose.

01 / Positioning

Brand and customer proposition.

02 / Discovery

Collection and product structure.

Product trust content.
