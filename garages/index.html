Can you add online MOT booking?

Potentially. We first confirm your workshop system, integration options and availability rules. Otherwise we can build a clearly labelled request journey.

Yes, as part of an agreed enquiry or integrated journey. Any external vehicle-data service and usage cost must be confirmed in the scope.
