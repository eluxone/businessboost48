What guests need

Answer the questions that decide where they eat.

A restaurant website is often visited on a phone, close to the decision. It needs to create desire and remove uncertainty at the same time.

01 / Desire

Make the experience feel worth choosing.

Your food, atmosphere and point of difference should be obvious before a guest reaches a gallery or downloads a menu.

02 / Clarity

Put practical answers within reach.

Menu, dietary information, location, opening hours, accessibility and group details should be easy to find on a small screen.

03 / Action

Make the booking route unmistakable.

Whether you use a booking platform, phone or enquiry form, the right next step should remain visible and easy to complete.
